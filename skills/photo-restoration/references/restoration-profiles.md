# Restoration Profiles

## Conservative
Deblur, denoise, remove scratches, correct exposure, preserve monochrome/era. No colorization or facial redesign.

## Portrait
Recover eyes, skin texture, and facial edges only where evidence exists. Preserve age, expression, asymmetry, hairstyle, and clothing.

## Colorization
Add plausible period-appropriate color while retaining original luminance, composition, skin identity, and objects. Label colorized output as interpretive.

## Document/photo with text
Preserve all text exactly. If readability is critical, produce an OCR/transcription companion and do not hallucinate missing characters.

## Upscale
Increase resolution while preserving all content and geometry. Do not crop or use a new composition.
