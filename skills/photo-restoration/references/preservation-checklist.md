# Preservation Checklist

Compare source and result side by side. Check identity and facial proportions, expression, number of people, pose, body proportions, clothing and accessories, object count, background geometry, camera angle, crop, text, and historical period. Record `pass`, `warn`, or `fail` with a short observation for each. A result fails if it invents a person, changes a recognizable object, removes required text, or changes the composition without permission.

For severe damage, mark uncertain regions explicitly. Prefer a conservative result and disclose that missing information was reconstructed rather than claiming historical accuracy.
